# Working with incomplete quotations

## Operator workflow

1. If extraction could not classify an uploaded or emailed PDF, register it as a
   quotation draft from the retained intake document. Confirm its project and
   contractor through the existing intake controls. Do not upload duplicate copies.
2. Open **Review/edit quotation**. Edit the French header or add a context-only
   row containing real source wording. Use **Fill empty English fields** or enter
   English yourself. Save wording and context without confirming financial totals.
3. A later re-scrape can prepare a candidate even after working edits. Use
   **Compare recovered content (optional)** in the editor. Select the source
   passage and its intended destination, then append the wording explicitly.
   Existing English, figures, images and stable row identities remain unchanged.
   Saving records the candidate's source provenance in the correction history.
4. To develop the client budget before every line is extracted, choose
   **Review indicative PDF totals**. Open the original, enter HT/TTC and the page
   containing the totals, confirm the comparison, review competing alternatives,
   and save the selected client-facing budget snapshot.
5. Before financial approval/signing, complete the ordinary original-source,
   actual-figures and translation checks. An indicative budget selection does
   not satisfy these checks and does not publish the full quotation.

## Safety boundaries

- Original PDF/extraction evidence remains immutable after source confirmation.
- Unknown intake placeholders are not quoted as known amounts.
- Budget snapshots retain source provenance internally; public responses omit
  those internal details and explicitly label reviewed totals as indicative.
- Source replacement invalidates a source-specific indicative snapshot.
- Archived, void, declined and superseded records cannot acquire this disclosure.
- Signed commitments remain separate; indicative overrides cannot change them.
- Stale working drafts and budget snapshots require refresh/conflict resolution.
- An incomplete/empty AI response does not count as a successful translation.
- Full candidate replacement remains protected when human or financial evidence
  exists. Wording comparison is not permission to overwrite that evidence.
