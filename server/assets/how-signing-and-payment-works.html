How signing and payment works on your project

Your architect uses ArchiTrak to manage quotations, signatures and payments on your building project. This one-page guide explains what happens at each step — and, most importantly, when to pay and when not to.

1
You receive a quotation to sign electronically

You'll get an email from Archisign, our secure e-signature service, with a link to review the contractor's quotation (the devis). Your architect has already checked it — including the contractor's insurance cover — before it reaches you.

2
Signing only authorises the work — it is not a request to pay

By signing, you approve the quotation so the contractor can be engaged. The quotation may show prices, deposits or bank details, but none of these are payable at this stage.

3
Pay only against a Payment Certificate

As work progresses, your architect verifies what has actually been done and issues you a Payment Certificate — a document stating exactly what to pay, to whom, and why. Every payment on your project is authorised this way, including any deposit.

4
Everything is tracked for you

ArchiTrak keeps a running record of what has been quoted, signed, certified and paid — so you always know where your project stands, and nothing is paid twice or too early.

!
Don't pay anything now.

At the signing stage, you are only authorising the quotation. Payment instructions will follow with each Payment Certificate. If you're ever unsure whether a payment request is genuine, check with your architect first.

What to expect

  • Signature requests always come via Archisign, with your architect's message.
  • Payment Certificates come from your architect, with clear instructions.
  • You can ask your architect about any document at any time.

What we will never do

  • Ask you to pay directly from a quotation or signature email.
  • Change bank details by email without a Payment Certificate.
  • Rush you — take the time you need before signing.
