Important correction: suppliers are not contractors
Our original misunderstanding was treating suppliers as contractor-shaped records.

The correct model is:

Contractors continue to use the existing contractor and devis workflows unchanged.
Direct-payment suppliers come exclusively from ArchiDoc Partners → Suppliers, backed by ff_suppliers.
ArchiTrak must identify them using the immutable opaque paymentSupplierId.
ArchiTrak must not create a contractor record for Richardson or any other supplier.
Legacy contractor-backed supplier history is audit-only and must not enter the active supplier feed.
A supplier quotation is stored separately from the existing contractor-backed devis records.
