**2. Value Engineering & Savings Breakdown**

Package: Pool Hydraulics & Waterproof Liner
What can be removed: The 3D Touch premium textured liner (€15,858.53) can be downgraded. The basic hydraulic infrastructure and sealing elements are mandatory.
Alternative Approaches: Replace the 3D Touch liner with a smooth 150/100th (1.5mm) standard reinforced PVC membrane (e.g., Alkorplan 2000).
Trade-Offs: Loss of the relief texture and high-end stone-like aesthetic, but waterproofing integrity and lifespan remain identical.

Package: Pool Terrace Finishes & Edging
What can be removed: The Schlüter Ditra-Drain 8 uncoupling mat (€6,053.23) and Schlüter-Bara edge profiles (€2,476.80) are technical enhancements rather than code requirements.
Alternative Approaches: Bond tiles directly to the slope screed using C2E-S1 flexible adhesive and adequate expansion joints. Alternatively, replace the entire tile sub-assembly with a single decorative concrete finish (*béton désactivé*).
Trade-Offs: Removing Ditra-Drain increases long-term risk of tile joint cracking from moisture and freeze-thaw cycles. Switching to decorative concrete changes the natural stone aesthetic.

Package: House Terrace Reconstruction (157 m²)
What can be removed: The entire package (€65,659.90), or optional sub-layers including the Ditra-Drain mat (€8,629.23), Bara edge profiles (€1,270.15), and travertine paving (€28,263.05).
Alternative Approaches: Defer house terrace tiling, or pour a single decorative exposed aggregate concrete slab (*béton désactivé*) directly over prepared ground, eliminating separate screeds, drainage mats, and tiles.
Trade-Offs: Removing the package leaves damaged areas around pipe trenches unrepaired. Decorative concrete creates a distinct visual contrast between the pool terrace and house perimeter, but delivers massive cost reductions.

Package: Technical Room Equipment & Automation
What can be removed: The Poolcop Evolution 2.0 automated system (€8,937.75), Zodiac Z400 iQ Heat Pump (€6,890.22), and submerged automatic cover (€16,130.28) are non-essential luxury items.
Alternative Approaches: Use standard manual chemical testing and timer-based filtration controls. Omit the heat pump (keeping bypass valves for future install). Replace the submerged cover with an above-ground motorized safety cover.
Trade-Offs: Manual pool management requires regular chemical testing and filter cleaning. Removing the heat pump limits pool usage to peak summer months. Above-ground safety covers impact surrounding lawn aesthetics.
