---
name: Intake duplicate recovery
description: Duplicate upload evidence must be distinguished from a live financial workflow record.
---
The user requires duplicate notices to identify and open the actual workflow destination, not merely name a previous intake upload. A retained source is not proof that its devis or invoice remains live.

**Why:** Re-uploading a required quotation was blocked by an older intake copy whose quotation had since been voided, with no usable navigation or recovery action.

**How to apply:** Preserve void history and immutable financial evidence. Recovery is an explicit, audited new draft or resumption of the canonical unpromoted source—not an automatic unvoid, a global dedup bypass, or a second independent job for every uploaded copy.
