---
name: Agreed worksheet sharing & revision requests
description: Policies for sealed client worksheet copies, client comments, contractor revision requests and revised-quotation comparison.
---

Rule: the client only ever sees a sealed, immutable copy of a chosen worksheet version, and at most one version per worksheet is published (sharing a new one replaces the old; stopping withdraws all). Later edits reach the client only when the architect shares again. Sharing requires complete approved English wording for every contractor line; it is refused otherwise. Copies disappear when the quotation is void/provisional/superseded.

Revision requests go to the contractor in French and record the agreed lines as sent. Comparison with the revised quotation uses the latest request the contractor actually received; pending/failed later requests are flagged, never used as the basis. Line notes are opt-in for the contractor because they were written for the client meeting.

**Why:** the client must never see unreviewed or untranslated content, and a comparison must reflect what the contractor was really asked for.

**How to apply:** any new client-facing worksheet view reads the sealed copy, never the live worksheet; any new comparison surface keeps the delivered-request basis and recomputes on open (the app cache never goes stale on its own).
