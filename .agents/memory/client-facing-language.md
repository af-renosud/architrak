---
name: Client-facing language
description: Language policy for outbound client communications.
---

All system-generated text in outbound client emails must be written in English, including subjects, greetings, instructions, consequences, and sign-offs. Internal operator-facing UI and audit text may remain in French where that is the established convention.

**Why:** The firm's clients are English-speaking; a French quotation-link email reached a client and was explicitly rejected.

**How to apply:** Whenever adding or editing an outbound client template, assert the complete generated subject and body in English and include a regression check against accidental French system copy.
