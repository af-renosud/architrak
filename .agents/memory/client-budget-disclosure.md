---
name: Client developing-budget disclosure
description: Architect-selected unsigned budget summaries are separate from detailed quotation publication and signed commitments.
---

The client may see a developing works budget composed of signed commitments plus unsigned quotations explicitly selected by the architect. Budget selection exposes only a reviewed title, reference, contractor and HT/TTC amounts; it does not authorise disclosure of the full quotation, translation, context or internal evidence.

**Why:** The user wants clients to understand the emerging cost profile before quotations are ready for detailed review or signature, while retaining explicit control of disclosure.

**How to apply:** Keep signed commitments and their invoicing/progress calculations unchanged. Never include project-wide unsigned prices by default. A budget selection is a separate disclosure authorisation, not a translation approval.

Unsigned shared amounts remain the architect's saved snapshot until an explicit budget update. Signed selections move out of unsigned totals, avoiding double-counting. Void, provisional, superseded and declined quotations are excluded; replacements require fresh inclusion. Alternative quotations must not be summed as additional scope; require the architect to confirm the preferred option.

**Why:** Routine extraction/editing must not silently change a figure the client is already using to understand their budget, and replacement or competing proposals must not inflate it.

**How to apply:** Version and audit selection changes, retain prior amounts on unconfirmed edits, and explain that the indicative total is not a contract or necessarily the complete project cost. Selections are project-scoped and survive link rotation; link revocation still denies access to the whole portal.

The user requires active signed quotations to appear automatically on the project client link, without a separate selection. This is a requested product rule, not evidence that current publication membership implements it.

**Why:** Signed work can already contribute to financial summaries while its quotation is absent from the detailed list, which confuses the client and architect.

**How to apply:** Keep unsigned budget disclosure and full-document sharing explicit and distinct. Automatic signed visibility must not expose later unreviewed edits or resurrect void/replaced quotations; show the signed record rather than treating all working content as approved.
