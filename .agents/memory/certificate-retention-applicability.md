---
name: Certificate retention applicability
description: Distinguish calculation defaults from agreed retention, especially for professional services.
---

A default retention rate is not evidence that retention was agreed. Keep an explicit operator decision for no retention or a custom amount; do not infer exemptions from contractor names or service categories.

**Why:** The user highlighted that retention often should not apply to engineering, geotechnical support and surveying. They requested a prominent red review box and override, not a change to automatic retention policy.

**How to apply:** Explain whether a retention amount comes from an application default, marché configuration, bank guarantee or operator override. For professional services, prompt a check of the agreed terms rather than silently changing money.
