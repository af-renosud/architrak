---
name: Translation review note privacy
description: Line-item questions shown in translation editing are internal or contractor-only references.
---
Line-item review notes/questions may be displayed alongside their corresponding translation lines solely to inform the architect. The user explicitly confirmed they must not be included in any translation or any outbound client-facing documentation.

**Why:** These questions belong to internal review and direct contractor communication. Seeing them while preparing a translation must not turn them into client content.

**How to apply:** Keep this reference display separate from client-facing Context fields and translation content. Never automatically copy, feed into translation generation, or export the notes into client PDFs, portals, emails or signature packages. Preserve the existing contractor communication workflow.
