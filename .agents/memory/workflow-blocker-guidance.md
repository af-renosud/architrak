name: Workflow blocker guidance
description: Explain blocked actions at the point of use without weakening workflow safeguards.
---
Blocked workflow actions must explain why, identify the prerequisite, and offer a direct route to the relevant field or section when one exists. Support keyboard and touch, not just hover.

**Why:** The user found inactive stage buttons unintelligible and approved contextual help, persistent action-specific checklists and targeted highlighting.

**How to apply:** Keep approval prerequisites separate from sending prerequisites. Distinguish missing input, waiting, read-only restrictions and technical failures. Never treat an unavailable prerequisite check as clearance. Preserve drafts, retain server validation, and never automatically execute an action when its last blocker clears.

The architect may explicitly authorize signing despite unresolved contractor questions, without resolving or dismissing those questions.

**Why:** The user stated that outstanding questions are not always material to signing and that the architect should decide.

**How to apply:** Keep the default question blocker; require deliberate authorization scoped to the current questions, retain an audit reason and identity, and preserve all other signing safeguards.
